# Rentcars na página inicial

A seção substitui o banner compacto por uma composição editorial com a largura e o espaçamento das outras faixas da página inicial. Rentcars aparece como nome da parceira e no botão de reserva. O link de afiliada, a abertura em nova aba e o rastreio no contexto `home` são preservados.

A ilustração é decorativa e não representa um veículo ofertado pela locadora. Não são exibidos preços, descontos ou garantias de reserva.

## Arte

- Arquivo: `public/rentcars-viagem.webp`, 960 × 640px, com transparência.
- Gerada com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen e otimizada em WebP.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: decorative editorial road-trip illustration for the Rentcars affiliate section of Brazilian travel website Por Aqui Pelo Mundo. Create ONE high-end watercolor and fine gouache illustration with genuinely transparent alpha background, wide landscape 3:2 composition. A small elegant contemporary muted olive-green crossover rental car, three-quarter front view, on a quiet winding warm sand country road that curves gently into a few softly painted olive rolling hills. Beside the car one slim botanical branch and subtle wild grasses. Restrained distant terracotta sun. The car must be modern and tasteful, no vintage camper, no luggage piled on roof. Charming refined travel-journal art, tactile pigment and paper texture within painted areas, delicate brushwork, airy composition, subtle natural dimensional shadows, sophisticated editorial travel stationery mood. Full car visible, coherent realistic wheels and perspective, illustration not photorealistic. Palette limited to olive #4A5D43, warm sand #F0E6D2, muted terracotta #C1653A, ink #2B2620, ivory highlights. Road and hills taper organically to transparent edges, no rectangular background. No logos, readable text, rental-company marks, license plate text, people, frame, watermark, buttons, UI, gradients, heavy shadows or clipart. Preserve a modest transparent margin on all edges. Intended display width 480px on warm ivory website.
